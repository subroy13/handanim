Background Music & Automatic Chalk / Eraser SFX¶

voiceanim includes a 100% open-source procedural sound synthesis engine (voiceanim.sfx) and multi-track soundtrack mixer (voiceanim.mixer) integrated directly with render().

1. Repeating Background Theme Music¶

Add repeating background music to any handanim video in two ways:

Option A: Directly in Scene or scene.render(...)¶

Pass a built-in open-source theme preset ("marimba", "calm", "upbeat"), True, an AudioClip, or a path to your own .wav / .mp3 file:

from handanim import Scene

scene = Scene(width=1080, height=1920, fps=30)
# Configure on the scene:
scene.set_background_music("marimba", volume=0.18, loop=True)

# Or pass directly to scene.render():
scene.render(
    "video_with_music.mp4",
    narration=clip,
    background_music="marimba",  # Loops seamlessly & auto-ducks under speech!
    music_volume=0.18,
    music_loop=True,
)

When narration is also provided, mix_soundtrack() automatically ducks the background music volume while the narrator is speaking so the voice stays crystal clear.

Option B: Standalone via voiceanim Functions¶

Use generate_theme_music() or loop_audio() (also available on VoiceEngine) to generate or repeat any audio clip to a target duration:

from voiceanim import VoiceEngine, generate_theme_music, loop_audio

# Generate 30 seconds of repeating marimba theme music
theme_clip = generate_theme_music(duration=30.0, style="marimba", bpm=104.0, volume=0.22)

# Or loop any short WAV/MP3 file seamlessly for 30 seconds
looped_clip = loop_audio("my_short_loop.wav", duration=30.0, volume=0.20)

2. Automatic Chalk Sketch Sound During SketchAnimation¶

When sketch_sound=True is enabled on Scene (or passed to scene.render(..., sketch_sound=True)), handanim automatically scans the timeline for all SketchAnimation events, merges overlapping intervals, and synthesizes a realistic chalk / sketch-pen drawing sound (synthesize_chalk_sound()) synchronized to every stroke reveal:

# Configure chalk or felt-pen style and volume on the scene
scene.enable_sketch_sound(enabled=True, volume=0.45, style="chalk")  # style="chalk" or "pen"

3. Automatic Board-Wiping Sound During Eraser¶

When eraser_sound=True is enabled (or passed to scene.render(..., eraser_sound=True)), any Eraser animation automatically plays a realistic back-and-forth felt/rubber board-erasing sound (synthesize_eraser_sound()) matched to the duration of the wipe:

from handanim import Eraser, SketchAnimation

scene.enable_eraser_sound(enabled=True, volume=0.55)

eraser = Eraser(objects_to_erase=[diagram])
scene.add(SketchAnimation(start_time=5.0, duration=0.9), eraser)

scene.render("erased_demo.mp4", sketch_sound=True, eraser_sound=True)

4. Automatic Page-Turn & Paper-Crumple Sounds (transition_sound=True)¶

When transition_sound=True is enabled on Scene (via Scene(..., transition_sound=True), enable_transition_sound(), or scene.render(..., transition_sound=True)), handanim automatically synthesizes procedural paper sound effects timed to every SceneTransition:

  • PageTurnTransition triggers synthesize_page_turn_sound() (a subtle corner-lift flick, swept paper-on-paper sliding friction, and a soft crease-settling pat).

  • PaperCrumpleTransition triggers synthesize_crumple_sound() (a dense cascade of dry paper-crinkle micro-bursts and stiff paper snaps during the crunch phase, followed by an aerodynamic whoosh as the crumpled ball is tossed aside).

from handanim import PageTurnTransition, PaperCrumpleTransition, Scene

scene = Scene(sketch_sound=True, transition_sound=True)
scene.enable_transition_sound(enabled=True, volume=0.55)

scene.add(PageTurnTransition(start_time=3.0, duration=1.2, direction="bottom_right"))
scene.add(PaperCrumpleTransition(start_time=6.5, duration=1.4, direction="right"))

scene.render("notebook_story.mp4")

5. Additional Procedural SFX (pop, whoosh, ding, page_turn, crumple)¶

You can also pass custom timed sound effects via sfx_events to render() or mix_soundtrack():

scene.render(
    "full_sfx.mp4",
    sketch_sound=True,
    eraser_sound=True,
    transition_sound=True,
    sfx_events=[
        {"type": "pop", "start_time": 1.2, "volume": 0.5},
        {"type": "whoosh", "start_time": 3.0, "duration": 0.4, "volume": 0.45},
        {"type": "ding", "start_time": 4.5, "volume": 0.5},
    ],
)
